## Platform-Specific Adjustments

### Twitter (280 characters)
- Be concise and punchy
- Use 1-3 hashtags max
- Thread for longer content
- Include emojis for visual appeal

### LinkedIn (3000 characters max)
- More professional tone
- Can be longer and detailed
- Use line breaks for readability
- Include industry-specific hashtags
- Professional emojis okay

### Instagram (2200 characters)
- Visual-first mindset
- More casual/personal tone
- Multiple hashtags (10-30)
- Emojis throughout
- Stories for behind-scenes

### Facebook
- Conversational tone
- Medium length (100-250 words ideal)
- Questions for engagement
- Mix of text and visual elements

## AI Prompt Enhancements

### For Better Content Generation
Add these elements to your AI prompts:

1. **Tone**: "Write in a [professional/casual/inspiring] tone"
2. **Length**: "Keep it under [X] characters"
3. **Elements**: "Include a question, insight, and call-to-action"
4. **Style**: "Write like [expert/friend/mentor]"
5. **Format**: "Use short paragraphs and bullet points"

### Example Enhanced Prompt
```
You are a thought leader in technology and entrepreneurship. 
Write an engaging LinkedIn post (under 300 words) that:
- Starts with a compelling question or statistic
- Shares a practical insight or tip
- Ends with a call-to-action
- Uses professional but approachable tone
- Includes relevant hashtags

Topic: [Your topic here]
```

## Content Mix Strategy

### The 80-20 Rule
- 80% valuable content (education, inspiration, entertainment)
- 20% promotional content (products, services, offers)

### Content Pillars (Example)
1. **Education** (40%): Tips, how-tos, tutorials
2. **Inspiration** (30%): Quotes, stories, motivation
3. **Entertainment** (20%): Humor, memes, light content
4. **Promotion** (10%): Products, services, offers

## Quality Checklist

Before posting, ensure:
- [ ] Content is original and valuable
- [ ] Spelling and grammar are correct
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant
- [ ] Tone matches platform and audience
- [ ] Call-to-action is clear
- [ ] Length is appropriate for platform
- [ ] No sensitive or controversial topics
- [ ] Emojis enhance, not distract
- [ ] Post timing is optimal
- [ ] Content aligns with brand voice
